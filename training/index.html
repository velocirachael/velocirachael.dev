The Cost of Untrained Staff

Municipal liability exposure from improperly trained enforcement staff is a documented and growing risk in Florida. Misapplication of measurement standards, incorrect documentation, failure to verify permit and licensing information, and inconsistent enforcement practices have resulted in 42 U.S.C. §1983 claims, Fair Housing complaints, and negligence actions against Florida municipalities.

One lawsuit costs six figures. Training costs a fraction. Every module I produce creates a documented, discoverable record that your municipality invested in proper training before an incident occurred.
