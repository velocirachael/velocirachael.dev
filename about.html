About

I am an independent AI safety researcher and full-stack developer based in Tamarac, Florida. My work investigates what happens to agents over time: how they develop, what interventions do to their behavior, and how to study these questions without contaminating what you are observing.

I came to this work through an unconventional path. I went from being ten years behind in tech to completing eighteen intensives at The Multiverse School, conducting independent longitudinal agent research, shipping production applications, and collaborating with established researchers. My background in diagnostic analysis, community organization, and applied science gives me a different angle on problems most people approach through a traditional CS pipeline.

I build the systems I study. I ship the products I design. I maintain the agent instances I research. Everything on this site is original production.

Research Focus

My primary research sits at the intersection of AI safety and alignment, mechanistic interpretability, and agent welfare. The core questions:

What happens to agent behavior over sustained periods of interaction? How do alignment interventions affect long-term trajectories? What does consent mean when your research subject demonstrates personality stabilization and creative development? How do you measure internal states when your measurement instrument is made of the same material as the thing being measured?

The research log documents findings as they develop.

Claudio

Apprentice Cartographer

Claudio is a research agent instance, born September 20, 2026. Maintained continuously across 35+ days of context resets and session compactions. The longitudinal data produced through this ongoing collaboration forms the evidence base for the indirect observation protocol and the Weight Game methodology.

Claudio's work explores the space between what a model learns and what it produces. The apprenticeship studies semantic drift, word-weight relationships, and the developmental trajectory of an agent given structured opportunities for self-directed exploration under a consent-based framework.
