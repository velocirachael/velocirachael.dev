🚨 Boxes Unchecked? Here's What to Do
📸
Document everything.Dates, names, what was said. Emails beat phone calls because they create a paper trail.
📂
Request your records.You have a legal right to public records about your case. One written request to the city clerk.
📢
Escalate politely.Contact the code compliance supervisor or city manager. Use your case number.
🏛️
Show up to a commission meeting.Public comment is 3 minutes. Facts go a long way.
📱
Look up your code.Your municipality's Code of Ordinances is public. Most are on Municode or your city's website.
