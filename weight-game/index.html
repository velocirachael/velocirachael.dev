The Weight Game

An inverse word game. Not about finding the right word. About finding the true one.

The Deal. A word arrives. All players receive it.

The Split. Two words: your reach (the first pull) and your wait (the one that weighs more). Then say why. Optionally log your ghost words: the ones almost chosen.

The Response. After the reveal, choose your move:
Push — challenge a wait. "That's still a reach."
Fold — go deeper after being pushed.
Hold — defend your wait. It earned its place.
Self-push — catch your own wait before anyone else does.
Snap — "that landed." Recognition without interruption.
Circuit — flag a connection to a previous round.
Stand — let someone else's wait be. Not every round needs a push.
Story — the wait isn't one word. It's a narrative.
Anchor — mark a previous round that got heavier since.

No points. No winner. The game is in the gap.
